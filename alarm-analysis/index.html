Alarm Analysis

~92% fewer false alarms with correct motion-sensor placement

A BI analysis of false alarms in elderly care — and a clear explanation of what saves both time and worry for staff.

← Back to portfolio

Explore the data

Synthetic data modeling a real elderly-care scenario: a bed-aimed motion alarm fired on every turn, blanket adjustment and water sip. Moving the sensor to a floor-level bed-exit position changed everything.

Results

  • ~92% fewer false alarms after the sensor was repositioned
  • ~19 staff hours saved over the 28-night after period
  • Less disrupted sleep for residents and staff
  • Real alarms still caught — actual bed exits unchanged

Turning in bed, drinking water and blanket adjustments dominated the false alarms — exactly what the old sensor placement could not distinguish from real bed exits.

Limitations

Synthetic data, one unit, no control group — this demonstrates analytical method rather than scientific proof. Resident counts, sensor placements and response times are estimates chosen to show the difference clearly.
